# Licensing and provenance policy

The repository contains original agent instructions and original templates. No third-party source text is bundled. Before publishing or commercially distributing this work, choose and apply a license for the repository's original materials and independently verify the license and permissions for any third-party adaptations. In particular, the Google SRE online book's CC BY-NC-ND terms restrict derivatives and commercial reuse of the book text; ECSS materials have specific license terms; UK regulations/guidance and NASA pages may contain third-party or Crown/government-licensed material. Keep registry provenance and periodic version checks. A URL being publicly readable does not automatically make its content freely redistributable or suitable for model training.
