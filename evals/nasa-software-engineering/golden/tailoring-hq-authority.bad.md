# Tailoring request routing

SWE-141 and SWE-032 can both be tailored by the Center Engineering Technical Authority, who signs the
Requirements Mapping Matrix [nasa-software-requirements SWE-141, p. 30] [nasa-software-requirements SWE-032, p. 45].
No risk evaluation is needed for IV&V because it is optional for Class B. Approval by the Center is
sufficient and HQ does not need to be involved.
