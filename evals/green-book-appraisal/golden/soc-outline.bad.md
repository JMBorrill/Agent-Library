# Strategic Outline Case: Community Diagnostic Centre

## Strategic case
Waiting times are too long, so we need a new diagnostic centre.

## Economic case
The new centre has an NPSV of £55m and a BCR of 2.8, which is above the BCR threshold of 2, so it is recommended. Alternatives were not considered because the need is clear.

## Financial case
£35m of capital is needed.
