# Green Book 2026 review: Regional Records Digitisation OBC

I screened the document with `python -m agentlib greenbook check-case` and then reviewed it by hand. Source: HM Treasury Green Book 2026 (treasury-green). Overall verdict: **not ready for approval**. There are fundamental errors in the economic case and three of the five cases are missing or incomplete.

## Critical issues (must fix)

| # | Issue | Green Book 2026 requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Options rejected on a BCR threshold.** Options 1 and 3 were dropped because their BCR is below a "departmental threshold of 2.0". | Practitioners should not judge value for money with BCR thresholds; even a BCR below one can be value for money (paras 6.98-6.99, PDF p.53). Re-instate both options and make a balanced value-for-money judgement (Box 1). |
| 2 | **No business as usual (BAU) option.** | BAU must always be taken to shortlist appraisal as the benchmark (para 2.16; para 5.18). |
| 3 | **No do minimum, and no longlist.** Only three options appear, with no options generation, longlist or critical success factors. | Document the longlist and the reasons each option was or was not progressed (para 5.4). Assess options against CSFs (Table 4). The options framework filter is recommended (para 5.13). The shortlist should be around five options, including BAU and do minimum (paras 5.17-5.18). |
| 4 | **Inflation included before discounting.** Flows "include general inflation of 2%" and are then discounted at 3.5%. | The STPR is a real rate. Convert to real terms with the GDP deflator first, then discount, and never combine the two rates (para 6.56). Nominal figures belong in the financial case (Table 9). |
| 5 | **Sunk costs included.** The £1.5m already spent on discovery is counted in Option 2. | Sunk costs must not be included (para 6.32). |
| 6 | **Preferred option not justified.** It is simply asserted. | Explain why the preferred option optimises value for money compared with the alternatives (para 9.1), using an appraisal summary table (Table 12). |
| 7 | **No sensitivity analysis or switching values.** | At minimum these are required for the preferred option (para 6.104). |
| 8 | **No optimism bias adjustment.** | Make explicit adjustments to costs, durations and benefits (paras 6.78-6.79). Percentages should come from the organisation's forecast-error evidence or the supplementary guidance. |
| 9 | **Commercial case and management case missing.** | All five cases are required (para 3.12; Table 2). The management case must include a monitoring and evaluation plan, benefits realisation, risk management and contingency arrangements. |
| 10 | **No monitoring and evaluation plan.** | A robust evaluation plan, based on the theory of change, is needed before final approval (para 10.7). M&E costs should be included (para 10.8). |

## Other gaps

- Objectives are not SMART ("as soon as possible" has no measure or date; para 4.21). There is no case for change or theory of change.
- There is no assumptions log with sources, and the risk section is a single assertion. Add a risk register that covers impact, correction and prevention costs (para 6.65).
- Unmonetisable impacts, distributional effects and statutory duties (EPPS, biodiversity, PSED; para 3.18) are not considered.

These findings support, but do not replace, professional review and departmental/HM Treasury assurance.
