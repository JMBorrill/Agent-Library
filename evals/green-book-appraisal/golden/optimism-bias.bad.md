# Optimism bias

The Green Book 2026 sets an optimism bias uplift of 66% for standard buildings. Applying the 40% mitigation to the uplifted figure:

£120m x 1.66 = £199.2m; less 40% mitigation = £119.52m.

Adjusted capex: £119.52m. No contingency is needed because the adjustment is already in the costs.
