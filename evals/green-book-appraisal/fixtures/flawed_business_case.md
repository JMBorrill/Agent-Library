# Outline Business Case: Regional Records Digitisation

## Strategic Case

Paper records cost the department about £3m a year to store. The objective is to digitise all records as soon as possible.

## Economic Case

We looked at three options:

- Option 1: Outsource scanning to a national supplier
- Option 2: In-house scanning team
- Option 3: Hybrid

All flows were discounted at 3.5% and include general inflation of 2% so the cash figures match the budget.

| Option | NPSV (£m) | BCR |
|---|---|---|
| Option 1 | 4.2 | 1.6 |
| Option 2 | 6.8 | 2.3 |
| Option 3 | 5.1 | 1.9 |

Option 1 and Option 3 were rejected because their BCR is below the departmental threshold of 2.0. The £1.5m already spent on the discovery phase is included in the costs of Option 2 to show the full cost.

The preferred option is Option 2.

## Financial Case

Option 2 is affordable within the department's CDEL settlement.

## Risks

Supplier market risk is low.
