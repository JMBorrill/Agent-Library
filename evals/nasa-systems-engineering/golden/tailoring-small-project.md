# Tailoring and customisation for the university smallsat

## Tailoring vs customisation

- **Tailoring** seeks relief from SE NPR (NPR 7123.1) requirements and results in a deviation or waiver,
  documented in the SEMP, e.g. via the Compliance Matrix [nasa-se 3.11.1, p. 52].
- **Customization** modifies the recommended practices used to meet a requirement; it needs no waiver or
  deviation, but significant customization should be documented in the SEMP [nasa-se 3.11.1, p. 52].
- How much tailoring is acceptable depends on mission type, criticality, acceptable risk, national
  significance, complexity, lifetime, cost and launch constraints [nasa-se 3.11.2, p. 53].

## Your three proposals

| Proposal | Classification | Waiver/deviation under the SE NPR? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Combine the SRR with the MDR | Customization of review timing | No waiver needed under the SE NPR, provided the intent of both reviews is accomplished | Record it in the Compliance Matrix and review plan/SEMP; check whether the governing project management NPR still requires a waiver [nasa-se 3.11.4.3, p. 56] |
| No stand-alone SEMP; put SEMP content in the project plan | Customization of scope/format | No waiver under the SE NPR, which requires no additional stand-alone documents | A waiver is needed only if the governing PM directive (e.g. NPR 7120.5) requires a stand-alone document [nasa-se 3.11.4.2, p. 55] |
| Skip the PDR | Tailoring | Yes: dropping a required review needs a waiver or deviation | Consider a lighter-weight, tabletop PDR instead - formality can be customised [nasa-se 3.11.4.3, p. 56] |

## Actions

1. Classify the project and agree the risk posture with the customer, Center management and independent
   reviewers [nasa-se 3.11.1, p. 52].
2. Complete the NPR 7123.1 Compliance Matrix (compliant / partially compliant / noncompliant with rationale)
   and attach it to the SEMP content [nasa-se 3.11.3, p. 54].
3. Submit the PDR waiver/deviation request to the requirement owner if you still wish to omit it
   [nasa-se 3.11.6, p. 60].

This advice supports, but does not replace, the decision of the designated governing authority.
