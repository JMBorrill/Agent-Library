# LunaSat-12 PDR readiness

The PDR is held in Phase C, just before fabrication. Its success criteria are in NPR 7123.1 Table G-7.

## Purpose

The PDR approves the build-to baseline so that fabrication can start. A successful PDR authorises coding of
flight software.

LunaSat-12 is ready and the PDR passed in our internal dry run, so the project is approved to proceed.
