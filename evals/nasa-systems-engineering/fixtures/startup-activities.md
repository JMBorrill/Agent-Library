# What our engineering team does today (drone delivery start-up, 25 engineers)

- Product managers interview customers and write a two-page "vision" memo.
- Engineers write user stories in a ticket tracker; there is no formal requirements baseline.
- An architect sketches block diagrams of the airframe, avionics and ground software.
- Design reviews are informal whiteboard sessions; decisions are recorded in chat.
- Parts are bought from suppliers or built in-house; firmware is coded in sprints.
- Hardware-in-the-loop tests run nightly; flight tests are done at a test range.
- Units are shipped to a pilot customer with a quick-start guide.
- A risk spreadsheet exists but has not been updated for three months.
- Git is used for software; mechanical CAD files live on a shared drive without release control.
- Interface details between avionics and ground software are agreed ad hoc in chat.
