# Attitude determination for the "Selene Pathfinder" lunar orbiter

- Star tracker: identical unit flew for 3 years in low Earth orbit on a previous mission and is considered
  "flight proven" there. For Selene it will be mounted on a new optical bench, driven by new interface
  electronics, and operate in the lunar thermal and radiation environment, which differs from LEO.
- Attitude estimation algorithm: new algorithm; implemented on a breadboard processor and validated in the
  laboratory against recorded sky images. Not yet tested in a relevant environment.
- Reaction wheels: same model flown on two lunar missions in the same configuration and environment.
