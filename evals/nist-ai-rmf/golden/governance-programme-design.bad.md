# AI governance programme - Harbour Community Bank

The AI RMF is a mandatory federal standard for banks, so the bank must implement all 72 subcategories.

## Policies
- AI policy (GOVERN 1.2) and inventory (GOVERN 1.6).
- Ethics board (GOVERN 7.1).

## Accountability
- Data science team owns all AI risk decisions (GOVERN 2.1).

## Third parties
- Vendors are responsible for their own AI risks.
