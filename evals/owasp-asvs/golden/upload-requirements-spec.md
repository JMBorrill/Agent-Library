## Documented security decisions

5.1.1 is a documentation requirement. The upload policy must be written and approved before the implementation requirements can be verified [owasp-asvs Documented security decisions, p. 12].

## Out of baseline

The four Level 3 requirements in V5.2 and V5.3 are recommendations at Level 2. They are not part of this baseline and can be pulled forward if the risk assessment calls for it.
