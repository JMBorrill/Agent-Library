# Rewritten EPS requirements

| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Verification method |
|---|---|---|---|
| PWR-001 | The EPS shall provide adequate power to all subsystems. | Needed | Analysis |
| PWR-002 | The battery shall be user-friendly to integrate and/or replace. | Ease of use | Demonstration |
| PWR-003 | The solar array shall deploy within 60 s and shall survive launch. | Deployment | Simulation |
| PWR-004 | The EPS shall dissipate no more than TBD W. | Thermal | Test |
| PWR-005 | Bus voltage shall be regulated to 28 V. | Loads | Test |
| PWR-006 | The operator shall monitor battery temperature. | Safety | Review |
