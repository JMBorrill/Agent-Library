# Allocation notes from the MDR (rover mobility)

Stakeholder needs: NGO-3 (traverse 5 km over the mission), NGO-4 (survive the lunar night).

- MOB-001 (system): The rover shall traverse at least 5 km over the mission on slopes up to 15 deg. Traces to NGO-3. Verified by test and analysis.
- MOB-002 (system): The rover shall survive at least 14 Earth days of lunar night at -170 C. Traces to NGO-4. Verified by analysis.
- WHL-001 (subsystem): Each wheel motor shall deliver at least 3 Nm continuous torque. Derived from MOB-001. Verified by test.
- WHL-002 (subsystem): The wheels shall have a grouser height of 10 mm +/- 1 mm. Derived from MOB-001. Verified by inspection.
- THM-001 (subsystem): The RHU heater set shall provide at least 20 W to the battery enclosure. Derived from MOB-002. Verified by analysis and test.
- THM-002 (subsystem): The battery enclosure insulation shall limit heat loss to no more than 18 W at -170 C. Derived from MOB-002. Verified by test.
