# ADCS requirements (SRD-ADCS rev B, section 3.2)

- ADCS-001 (3.2.1): The ADCS shall point the payload boresight to within 0.1 deg (3 sigma) of the commanded attitude.
- ADCS-002 (3.2.2): The ADCS shall detumble the spacecraft from 10 deg/s to below 0.5 deg/s within 3 orbits.
- ADCS-003 (3.2.3): The ADCS shall have a mass of no more than 1.8 kg.
- ADCS-004 (3.2.4): The ADCS shall carry a red "Remove Before Flight" tag on the reaction wheel lock pin.
- ADCS-005 (3.2.5): The ADCS shall allow the operator to command safe mode from the ground console within 5 seconds of a fault alarm.
- ADCS-006 (3.2.6): The ADCS should minimize power consumption. (goal)
