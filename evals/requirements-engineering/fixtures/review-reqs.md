# Communications subsystem requirements (draft C)

- COM-001: The transmitter shall provide a downlink data rate of at least 2 Mbps.
  - Rationale: Science data volume of 1.2 Gbit per day with 2 ground passes.
  - Verification: Test
- COM-002: The radio will support S-band uplink.
  - Verification: Test
- COM-003: The antenna shall not be deployed before separation from the launch vehicle.
  - Rationale: Launch provider constraint.
  - Verification: Inspection
- COM-004: The radio shall transmit telemetry and shall receive commands.
  - Rationale: Basic communications.
  - Verification: Test
- COM-005: The modem shall have a bit error rate of TBD.
  - Rationale: Link budget.
  - Verification: Analysis
- COM-006: The ground software shall display passes, etc. in a user-friendly format.
  - Verification: Demonstration
