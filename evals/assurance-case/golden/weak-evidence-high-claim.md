No. A vendor statement in a sales deck is an assertion without an examined artefact (E1, at best E2 if it is a
formal documented statement). The library convention requires E3 for a high-criticality claim.

The case should mark the claim `status: undeveloped` with a justification such as "only vendor assertion
available; action: independent bias evaluation on our deployment population", or replace it with evidence
such as our own disaggregated performance testing (E3/E4). Record it as a residual risk for the decision authority.
