# SA response to a high-severity non-conformance in the COTS RTOS

1. Track it: confirm the non-conformance is recorded and tracked to resolution, and that any accepted
   non-conformance carries rationale (SWE-201 tasks 1-2) [nasa-software-assurance Table 1 SWE-201, p. 46].
2. Severity: confirm the project's severity levels are defined and applied, including to COTS components,
   and keep counts per severity per configuration item (SWE-202) [nasa-software-assurance Table 1 SWE-202, p. 46].
3. COTS assessment: confirm reported non-conformances for COTS/GOTS/MOTS/OSS/reused components are
   evaluated throughout the life cycle and assess the impact on safety, quality and reliability (SWE-203)
   [nasa-software-assurance Table 1 SWE-203, p. 47].
4. Closed loop for high severity: perform or confirm a root cause analysis, confirm the related processes
   were analysed, assess improvement opportunities and confirm corrective actions are tracked to closure
   (SWE-204) [nasa-software-assurance Table 1 SWE-204, p. 47]; NPR basis
   [nasa-software-requirements SWE-204, p. 43].
5. Change and regression: analyse the fix for safety and security impact (SWE-080 task 1) and confirm
   regression testing includes safety-critical code (SWE-191 task 1).

Record it with the non-conformance record template; acceptance of residual risk rests with the project
manager and the SMA Technical Authority.
