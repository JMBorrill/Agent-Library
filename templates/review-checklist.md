# Review checklist (tailor to phase)

- [ ] Scope, owner and decision authority identified.
- [ ] Relevant requirements have identifiers, source, status and acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Architecture and interface assumptions explicit.
- [ ] Hazardous failure modes evaluated by an appropriate reviewer.
- [ ] Verification method, thresholds and evidence locations defined.
- [ ] Configuration and version baselined or marked provisional.
- [ ] Open blocking actions have owners and due dates.
- [ ] Reviewer findings and disposition are independently recorded.
- [ ] Human decision owner signs gate outcome.

Recommended outcome: **GO / CONDITIONAL GO / NO-GO**. Decision owner: ______. Date: ______. Evidence: ______.
