# Se Stakeholder Conops

## When to use
Create stakeholder expectations and a concept of operations for engineering projects; use when defining the mission, operating scenarios and success measures.

## Procedure
1. Identify stakeholders, decision owners and intended operators.
2. Separate the problem/mission need from proposed technical solutions.
3. Document operating scenarios, modes, environments, off-nominal conditions and lifecycle constraints.
4. List assumptions and unresolved conflicts as questions; do not manufacture stakeholder interviews.
5. Draft a ConOps that can be validated with the project owner.

## Required outputs
mission-brief.md, conops.md, stakeholder-map.md

## Evidence and quality gates
- Cite applicable source IDs from `sources/registry.json` and document specific sections where verified.
- Identify each assumption, unresolved question, owner and review status.
- No fabricated observations, approvals, compliance conclusions or test evidence.
- For safety-critical or regulated decisions, escalate to a qualified human reviewer.
