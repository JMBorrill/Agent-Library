# Concept of operations — DEMO low-gravity locomotion rig (synthetic)

## Mission need
Researchers need repeatable measurements of how a small robot moves in a reduced-gravity analogue so
that locomotion concepts for small-body surfaces can be compared before costly flight work.

## Stakeholders
Principal investigator (decision owner), test operators, lab safety officer, funding sponsor.
See stakeholders.csv. Needs are drafted from a planning meeting on 2026-09-02 [evidence: minutes-2026-09-02 (synthetic)].

## Operational scenarios
### Nominal scenarios
S1 Calibrate offload rig; S2 execute a single hop trial and log data; S3 export data set.
### Off-nominal and contingency scenarios
S4 E-stop during hop; S5 tether snag; S6 logger failure mid-trial — trial voided and repeated.

## Operational modes
Setup, armed, trial, safe, maintenance.

## Operating environment
Indoor lab, 15–25 °C, cable offload rig emulating ~1e-4 g vertical load (assumption to be verified).

## Constraints
Lab floor load 500 kg/m²; operator must be outside the guarded zone during trials.

## Measures of effectiveness
MOE-1 hop height repeatability (needs.csv).

## Assumptions
A1 cable offload rig friction can be characterised to within 5 %.

## Open questions
Q1 Which flight-relevant gravity level should the analogue target?
