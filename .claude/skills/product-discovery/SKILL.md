# Product Discovery

## When to use
Run evidence-led product discovery with GOV.UK service principles; use for customer problems, concept validation and MVP scope.

## Procedure
1. Identify target users and the complete problem they face.
2. Capture observed evidence separately from hypotheses and imagined customer quotes.
3. Propose risky assumptions and cheap, ethical experiments to test them.
4. Specify metrics, accessibility/security needs and a discovery-to-alpha decision.
5. Prioritise an MVP by problem value and evidence strength, not only feature novelty.

## Required outputs
product-brief.md, experiment-backlog.md, discovery-evidence.md

## Evidence and quality gates
- Cite applicable source IDs from `sources/registry.json` and document specific sections where verified.
- Identify each assumption, unresolved question, owner and review status.
- No fabricated observations, approvals, compliance conclusions or test evidence.
- For safety-critical or regulated decisions, escalate to a qualified human reviewer.

Source ID: `gov-service`. Check current edition/version and applicability in `sources/registry.json`.
