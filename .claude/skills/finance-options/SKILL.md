# Finance Options

## When to use
Analyse commercial options, unit economics and investment cases with transparent assumptions and sensitivities.

## Procedure
1. State the decision question, counterfactual and time horizon.
2. Separate source facts from assumptions; cite input dates and currencies.
3. Construct scenario-based cashflows including acquisition, service delivery, support, fixed costs and taxes where relevant.
4. Calculate NPV, payback, margins and cash runway where justified; test downside cases.
5. Document uncertainty, non-financial impacts and conflicts of interest; do not give financial guarantees.

## Required outputs
business-case.md, assumptions.csv, scenarios.csv

## Evidence and quality gates
- Cite applicable source IDs from `sources/registry.json` and document specific sections where verified.
- Identify each assumption, unresolved question, owner and review status.
- No fabricated observations, approvals, compliance conclusions or test evidence.
- For safety-critical or regulated decisions, escalate to a qualified human reviewer.
