## Verified content
From the official OSCAL catalog (groups -> controls -> controls):
- 4 practice groups: PO Prepare the Organization; PS Protect Software; PW Produce Well-Secured Software; RV Respond to Vulnerabilities.
- 19 practices: PO.1–PO.5; PS.1–PS.3; PW.1, PW.2, PW.4–PW.9; RV.1–RV.3. 42 tasks in total. Full IDs/titles/task IDs in `sources/extracts/nist-ssdf-practices.json`.
- No PW.3 practice in v1.1. Task statement prose notes: PO.1.3 "Formerly PW.3.1"; PW.1.3 "Formerly PW.4.3".
- Tasks carry reference mappings (e.g. SP 800-53 controls such as SA-15, SR-3 seen in PO.1.3).
- Back-matter links to csrc.nist.gov/Projects/ssdf and the SP 800-218 final publication page.
